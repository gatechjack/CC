# OptiTrade replication — Corpus coverage / freshness audit

Read-only audit of the local crypto bar corpus (`cc/data/*_scalping.db`).
Generated by `00_corpus_audit.py`. No writes to any DB.

## Headline findings

1. **Provenance is BYBIT perpetual futures, NOT Binance.** Every `source_files`
   row in all four DBs is a `BYBIT_<coin>USDT.P` TradingView export. There is
   **no Binance data on the box.** (Task spec said "Binance corpus" — flagged as
   a lead, not silently relabeled.)
2. **4h and 1d do not exist natively.** Every DB holds `1m / 3m / 15m / 30m / 1h`
   only. The requested `4h` and `1d` must be **resampled from the clean 1h series**
   (or the study restricted to native 3m/15m/1h).
3. **`trading_corp.db` holds no crypto OHLCV** (only polymarket/kalshi equity
   history) — corpus is entirely in the per-coin scalping DBs.
4. Native series that ARE present are **100% contiguous** (0 missing bars vs the
   nominal grid) with **zero null OHLC**.

## Freshness

Corpus was ingested May–Jun 2026. Last bar:
- BTC: **2026-06-19 01:15 UTC**
- ETH / SOL / XRP: **2026-06-26 19:xx UTC**

Today is 2026-07-31 → the corpus is **~35–42 days stale**. Acceptable for a
historical backtest study (not trading live off it), but the OOS window will end
in late June 2026, not "now".

## Short-history caution

- **3m** history is short: BTC ~82 days, ETH/SOL/XRP ~46 days. Signal counts should
  still be adequate, but IS/OOS split of 3m spans only a few weeks each.

## Extra series present (not required, noted)

- `btc_scalping.db.bars_3m_bitunix` — **Bitunix-native** 3m, 16,387 rows,
  2026-05-15 → 2026-06-18. The only actually-Bitunix-sourced series in the corpus;
  could serve as a venue cross-check for BTC 3m, but it is short.

  OHLCV columns are intact and are all this study uses.

## Reproducibility

- All reads are `mode=ro`. No WAL/journal sidecars → static, reproducible.
- SHA256 of each source CSV is recorded in each DB's `source_files` table.
