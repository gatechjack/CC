## Phase 2 — B-AE assignment/exercise monitoring (built; monitoring-only)

with the two changes you asked for.
- **Risk (pre-event):** SHORT calls at `DTE ≤ assignment_risk_dte` (default **1**) that are ITM
  (`spot ≥ strike`, spot via `broker.quote`). Fired **EOD (close−20 min scheduler pass)** so an ITM short
  expiring **today or tomorrow** is surfaced *before* expiry — not just on the 15:00 terminal pass.

  (additive); any non-zero → an event.

  plus `pmcc_assignment_risk` / `pmcc_assignment_detected` audits. The alert **states the manual remedy**:
  *exercise the LEAP to cover delivery, OR buy-to-close the assigned short stock.* Engine does NOT auto-act.

`AccountState(100k)`. **Carve-out** (`_combo_is_bp_consuming`): only a **fresh net-debit OPEN with no

correct for when an open is ever enabled.)
