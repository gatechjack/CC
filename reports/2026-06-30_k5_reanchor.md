## DRIFT SUMMARY

### DRIFT-1 · B14 EXTENDED — `Position` constructor mismatch in `kalshi.py:_fetch_positions` (NEW BUG, not in plan)

`kalshi.py:239-244` calls `Position(symbol=…, qty=…, avg_entry_price=…, market_value=…)` but `models.py:Position` (line 161) declares fields `account`, `symbol`, `qty`, `avg_price`, `opened_ts`, `extra`. The call:
- passes `avg_entry_price` (no such field — should be `avg_price`)
- passes `market_value` (no such field)
- omits required positional fields `account` and `opened_ts`

**Runtime effect:** every `_fetch_positions` call on a non-stub `KalshiBroker` raises `TypeError`, caught silently by the `except Exception` at line 245, so positions always return `[]` even with valid credentials. The plan only noted the pykalshi API field bug (`p.position` vs `p.position_fp`); this is an independent bug that must also be fixed in K5·1. **Both bugs live in `_fetch_positions`; one surgical fix covers both.**

### DRIFT-2 · D19 — `Secrets` dataclass field ordering shifted; env-load lines differ from plan

An `odds_api_key: str | None` field was inserted **between** the two Kalshi fields:
- Plan said `kalshi_api_key_id` at `:156`, now at **:176**
- Plan said `kalshi_private_key_pem` at `:163`, now at **:183**
- Plan said env materialization at `:355-357`, now at **:391** (`kalshi_api_key_id`) and **:393** (`kalshi_private_key_pem`)

Build implication: any targeted-hunk patch to `secrets.py` must use the current line numbers. The `Secrets` constructor also now has `odds_api_key` between the two Kalshi fields — any new `Secrets(…)` call in tests must include it or use keyword args.

### DRIFT-3 · H28/H29/H30 — Script paths are under `trading_corp/scripts/`, not top-level `scripts/`

The plan referenced `scripts/refresh_kalshi_watchlist_stats.py` and `scripts/seed_kalshi_watchlist_deep.py`. Actual paths:
- `trading_corp/scripts/refresh_kalshi_watchlist_stats.py`
- `trading_corp/scripts/seed_kalshi_watchlist_deep.py`
- `trading_corp/data/kalshi_apify_client.py`

These are invoked as `python -m trading_corp.scripts.refresh_kalshi_watchlist_stats` (package path). Any deploy runbook referencing bare `scripts/` paths will fail.

### DRIFT-4 · G26 — Kalshi watch sort and `_query_polymarket_watch_only_rows` line numbers drifted significantly (+103 lines each)

- Hardcoded Kalshi watch sort: plan said `:4766`, now at **:4869**
- `_query_polymarket_watch_only_rows`: plan said `:4804`, now at **:4907**
- `_KALSHI_WATCH_SORT_KEYS` whitelist: **does not exist yet** (only `_PM_WATCH_SORT_KEYS` at :4878 exists). The K5 build must add the kalshi whitelist — insertion point is immediately after `_PM_WATCH_SORT_KEYS` closes at line 4901, before line 4903.
