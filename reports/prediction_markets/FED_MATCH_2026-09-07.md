## The shape — the ODD one (event + bucket, no teams/players/date)
None of the mlb/ufc/tennis/structural/cs2/soccer shapes applies. The join is the FOMC **meeting** +
the rate-change **bucket**.
- **Kalshi** `KXFEDDECISION-{YYMON}-{BUCKET}`, five buckets per meeting (from `yes_sub_title`):
  `H0` = "Fed maintains rate" (no change) · `H25` = "Hike 25bps" (exactly 25) · `H26` = "Hike >25bps"
  (more than 25) · `C25` = "Cut 25bps" · `C26` = "Cut >25bps".
- **Polymarket** phrases the same as a Yes/No binary. The transform is a **semantic parse** of the
  title's direction + bps magnitude + the `+`/`>` modifier:
  `no change` / `0 bps` → H0 · `hike/increase/raise … 25` → H25 · `… 50/50+/75+` (>25) → H26 ·
  `cut/decrease/lower … 25` → C25 · `… 50/50+/75+` → C26.

## The coarse gate (a right-event-wrong-bucket is a STOP)
A phrasing that spans two buckets is **never placed**:
- `25+ bps` (spans =25 and >25) — the exact case Jack flagged.
- a hike/cut with **no magnitude** ("Fed rate hike?", "will the Fed cut?").
- a **count** market ("cut 3 times in 2026") or a multi-meeting **parlay** ("Pause·Pause·Pause").
Note the key subtlety: because Kalshi's buckets are `=25` and `>25`, a `50 bps` / `50+` / `75+` phrasing
maps **cleanly** to the `>25` bucket — only `25+` (or no magnitude) is genuinely coarse. Political /
Fed-chair / nomination / dissent / personnel markets are **excluded**. CPI is a separate deferred workstream.

## ★ The alias lesson applies even with no teams
The phrasing→bucket map is a **transform**, and a wrong bucket map would look internally coherent every
single time (the PSG precedent). So:
1. the parse reads the **explicit** bps + direction from the title — no inference;
2. coarse is gated **aggressively** (any magnitude ambiguity → skip);
3. `build_bucket_index` validates each Kalshi bucket-**CODE** against its **own `yes_sub_title`**
   (independent evidence) — a code/label mismatch (a Kalshi relabel) is **skipped, not trusted**.
