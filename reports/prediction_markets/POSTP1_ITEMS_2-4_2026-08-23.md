### ★ SURVIVORSHIP CAVEAT (critical — read the one-sided ROI as an UPPER BOUND, not a copy expectation)
A position becomes "two-sided" precisely when the first side sours and the whale adds the other leg (hedge /
loss-cap / scalp). So "one-sided" can be *the trades that never needed rescue* = survivorship. At **entry**, the
live matcher cannot know which entries will stay one-sided, so it cannot selectively copy the survivors. The
one-sided cost-ROI is therefore an **upper bound**; the true copyable expectation sits between the blended and
the one-sided figure.

The avg_price-sum of two-sided pairs distinguishes the mechanism (a simultaneous hedge/market-make prices to
sum ≤ 1.0 — a locked spread; a *sequential* loss-hedge added later prices to sum > 1.0):
- **BetMechanic:** pairs=2825, sum <0.98 = 49% (market-making), >1.02 = 36% (sequential loss-hedge), median 0.98
  → **mixed**; a genuine market-making book PLUS some loss-hedging → its one-sided slice is *partly* clean,
  *partly* survivorship. Real directional component, but +33% is optimistic.
- **FordBronco:** pairs=83, sum >1.02 = **67%**, median **1.07** → **inconsistent with simultaneous hedging;
  dominated by sequential (added the loser's other side at a worse combined price).** So FordBronco's one-sided
  +45% (n=36) is heavily survivorship → NOT a reliable copy signal.

**/closed-positions has NO entry timestamps → simultaneous-vs-sequential is UNDETERMINABLE here.** What would
settle it: `/activity` per-fill timestamps (does the whale enter one side, then add the other later, and at what
price?). Recommended before any live consideration of BetMechanic or Kh4mz4t.
