## FLAGGED (not fixed; bounded / defense-in-depth)
- **Proceeds-vs-revenue cross-check WARNs, never latches** (F3, LOW): by design -- Kalshi `revenue` excludes our
  order fees, so a fee-sized gap is expected; the first settlement is hand-inspected via the boot-scan log. A large
  divergence is a WARN, not a latch (a fee-related divergence should not disarm). Carry as a possible refinement.
- **/live groups by raw ticker (not UPPER)** while the settlement-close stores UPPER(ticker) (LOW, not-live-today):
  Kalshi tickers are canonically UPPER end-to-end, so entry and close tickers match in practice; boot_reconcile
  (the safety-critical consumer) UPPERs both. A non-canonical-case entry could leave a cosmetic /live phantom.
  Defense-in-depth (UPPER in `subdivision.live_positions`) is a pm_web change -> deferred to avoid scope creep into
  the engine deploy.
- **/portfolio/settlements read is `limit=200`, no cursor** (LOW): a held ticker whose settlement scrolls past the
  newest 200 before any scan is never booked -> the position stays journal-held -> boot_reconcile latches R-b
  (fails SAFE, human resolves; not a silent mask). Fine at ~20 orders/day; add a windowed cursor if volume grows.
