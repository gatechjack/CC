## 7. Q7 — what can bite between "engine restarted" and "first fill reconciled"

- **bitunix bounce:** the restart drops `bitunix_futures`/`bitunix_sfp` (24/7). If bitunix holds open positions, verify
  it re-seeds + reattaches cleanly; a brief coverage gap is the cost (Jack's heads-up). Not PM's bug but PM's restart.
- **Matcher swap:** the engine picks up the on-disk 3-dim matcher (was the old in-memory moneyline copy). If the on-disk
  file differs from what was box-scratched, behaviour changes silently — verify getsource/hash of the loaded matcher.
- **Boot-reconcile latches on a not-clean account:** if the driver runs `reconcile_account` at boot and ANY non-PM
  position remains on the account (exclusivity incomplete, or a stray kalshi_copy_trading fill mid-shutdown), it LATCHES
  -> disarmed -> no order. This is correct (safety), but it means exclusivity must be FULLY clean (0 non-PM positions)
  before arming, not just "kalshi_copy_trading disabled."
- **Empty/failed index or no signal:** if the Kalshi index fetch fails or SDTrading places no new moneyline entry in the
  window, no signal matches -> no order (safe, but the first order simply doesn't happen yet). MLB Saturday games make a
  signal likely within hours, not guaranteed within minutes.
- **Wrong-leg / illiquid strike:** a moneyline copy still resolves to a YES or NO leg; if the matched Kalshi strike is
  illiquid or unquoted, it skips (correct). The NO-leg cost (1−yes) must be right — the lens.
- **IOC 0-fill / benign no-fill:** the marketable IOC may 0-fill if the book moved past the limit ± slippage. Benign (no
  order, retry next signal); do NOT raise the slippage cap to force a fill.
- **Loud reject -> auto-disarm:** an auth/bad-request reject raises `OrderPlacementError` -> the R5 auth-failure /
  consecutive-error latch fires -> account disarmed + open positions flagged for manual exit. Verify the latch behaves.
- **Idempotency:** a crash between POST and journal-write (the K9 window) leaves a real position the journal doesn't know
  — this is EXACTLY what boot-reconcile (now built) catches on the next boot. The idempotency proof (item 6) exercises
  the durable-journal dedup across a deliberate restart.
- **Sign convention:** if unconfirmed and wrong, the fill's reconcile verdict inverts — a placed YES reads as a mismatch
  (spurious latch) or a placed NO mis-nets. The Q4 check must land BEFORE the reconcile in item 4b.
