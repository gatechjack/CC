## 4. What R8 measures — success and failure, stated BEFORE
NOT like-for-like BY DESIGN: Jack-MLB copies **moneyline + totals + spreads** from the two whales on **kalshi_jack**;
legacy `poly_kalshi_mlb` copies **moneyline win/loss only**, from the same two, on **Karen**. A count/PnL divergence
is EXPECTED and is not a defect. Window: ~2–3 MLB game-days, with at least one engine restart inside it.

- **SUCCESS** = over the window the new platform: (a) places on the RIGHT market/leg/side for every signal
  (the R7.g correspondence, now at volume, vs each whale's live Polymarket position); (b) NEVER double-orders
  (idempotency at volume); (c) respects caps and gates behave (skips/latches fire only when they should);
  (d) boot-reconcile stays CLEAN across at least one restart; and (e) on the OVERLAPPING moneyline market, Jack-MLB's
  DIRECTION agrees with legacy on the same whale. PnL is NOT a success metric.
- **FAILURE / STOP** = a wrong-market or wrong-leg order; a double order; a boot-reconcile mismatch that is a false
  alarm (or a real one masked); a gate failing open; or **moneyline direction disagreeing with legacy on the same
  whale** (a matcher/leg bug, not a design divergence).
