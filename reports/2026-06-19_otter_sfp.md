## A — SFP detector (real-time, close-confirmed, no look-ahead)
Mechanical, OHLC-only, known at the SFP bar's close (uses only prior-L bars for the swept level):
- **bull/long:** `bar low < min(low over prior L)` **AND** `bar close > that prior-low` (swept liquidity below, closed back above). Stop = sweep-wick-low − buffer.
- **bear/short:** `bar high > max(high over prior L)` **AND** `bar close < that prior-high`. Stop = sweep-wick-high + buffer.

The swept level (prior-L extreme) is the **liquidity / key-level proxy** — see "Key-levels caveat". No future bars enter the trigger → no repaint by construction (confirmed in B & E.3).

## B — model-free MFE/MAE (is the SFP catching the extreme?)
Median MFE/MAE % over horizons, L=10, entry=close, train+validate:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| buy  | ~+0.07 / −0.06 | … | … | **+0.14 / −0.12** |

**R:R ≈ 1:1, symmetric.** Contrast the divergence signal's +0.23 / −0.01 (R:R ~3.6–4.3). The SFP does **not** sit at a clean extreme — after a sweep, price is about as likely to keep going as to revert. This is the model-free tell that SFP-alone has no edge.

## E — repaint-honesty test (decisive)
The corpus `*_divergence` column **repaints**: it marks the pivot in hindsight; the live alert fires ~2–3 bars later (proven in the divergence-scalp study — 100% of fires are the ±3-bar extreme; edge dies at confirmation lag k≥2). So "divergence in [i−4, i]" at the SFP bar's close may be using a mark that is **not yet known**. A divergence marked at bar m is known at bar i only if `m + LAG ≤ i`. Slide a fixed-width (5-bar) window back by LAG: **LAG0 reproduces Step D; LAG≥3 = honest (confirmed-only).**

**E.1 — LAG sweep (every config the same shape):**

| config | LAG0 (Step D) | LAG3 (honest) |
|---|---|---|
| buy L20 buf0.1% R2.0 | tr +0.318 / va +0.432 / lb +0.625 | **tr −0.467 / va −0.793** / lb +1.054 (n15) |
| buy L10 buf0.1% R2.0 | tr +0.133 / va +0.285 / lb +0.599 | **tr −0.456 / va −0.793** / lb +1.156 (n18) |
| sell L10 buf0.1% R2.0 | tr +0.174 / va +0.470 / lb +0.981 | **tr −0.359 / va −0.249 / lb −1.171** |
| sell L20 buf0.1% R1.5 | tr +0.037 / va +0.597 / lb +0.464 | **tr −0.073 / va −0.520 / lb −1.234** |

The edge lives **entirely** in the unconfirmed (LAG0) window. Require a *confirmed* divergence and **train + validate go negative** in every case. The buy-side lockbox stays positive at LAG3, but on **tiny N (15–18) with train+validate negative** — by our standing rule (*a signal lockbox-positive but train-negative is rejected; you cannot select what failed the optimization set*) that is **not** a find. Sell-side lockbox is negative too.

```

              unconfirmed(0-2)=128 (64%)   confirmed(>=3)=71 (36%)
```

When a divergence is near an SFP it is **overwhelmingly on the same bar (age 0)** — and *nothing* at ages 1/2/3. The SFP bar **is** the divergence-pivot bar that the corpus marked in hindsight. At that bar's close, in real time, the divergence has not confirmed yet (needs 2–3 more bars). So Step D's "confluence" is the divergence column telling the SFP, in hindsight, that this exact bar is the reversal. Pure look-ahead — identical to the divergence-scalp artifact.

**E.3 — SFP-execution repaint re-check (headline buy L20 buf0.1% R2.0):** k0 +0.318 ≈ k1 +0.332, decays k2 +0.096 / −0.008. The SFP *execution* layer is clean; the contaminant is the divergence *filter*, exactly as E.2 shows.

## Key-levels caveat (carried forward)
The backtest uses the prior-L **swing extreme** as the liquidity/key-level proxy. AlexO's pink/grey boxes are **hand-drawn, redrawn 4–5×/day, not exportable** (drawn objects, not plotted series) → not historically reconstructable. The genuine forward test of the operator's setup needs the **real boxes captured forward** (a screenshot/JSON → levels extractor — the Pink Box backlog item), then SFP-at-box + a **non-repainting real-time reversal confirmation** (momentum/CVD cross at the swept level, *not* the divergence column). That, plus a non-bear regime and more N, is the only path that could turn this concept bot-able.
